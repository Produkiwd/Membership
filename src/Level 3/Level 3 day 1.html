Chapter 01 · Foundation

APT in 60 Seconds

The triangle. The three dimensions. The hero.

1.1 The APT Model
AI Performance Triangle

Three dimensions.
One human exponent.

The APT Model is the framework behind every successful AI deployment in business. It says: AI Capability alone does not produce Business Impact. Human capability — distributed across three independent dimensions — is the exponential amplifier.

01
AI Skills · The Hero
Execution Layer — translate intent into AI output.
02
Thinking Framework
Cognitive Layer — how you think when working with AI.
03
Domain Expertise
Reality Check — what you know that makes AI work in your world.

The three dimensions are multiplicative, not additive. Weakness in any one suppresses the whole. You cannot compensate for a zero with strength in the others.

1.2 The BI Formula · Why APT is the Exponent

APT does not stand alone — it sits inside a larger model that captures how organizations turn AI investments into measurable Business Impact. This is the context that makes APT matter:

BI = DX × (CAI)APT

The strategic narrative: you can buy the best AI and curate the finest data, but without investing in your people's AI capability, returns are linear at best. APT is the cheapest lever with the highest exponential payoff — and AI Skills is the dimension you can teach.

Chapter 02 · The Hero Dimension

AI-Skills of the APT Model

The Execution Layer — where intent becomes output.

2.1 What AI Skills Is

AI Skills is the hands-on, tactical capability to interact effectively with AI systems. It is the operational and technical fluency dimension of APT — the layer where human intent is translated into machine action.

Where Thinking Framework determines what to ask and Domain Expertise determines what is true, AI Skills determines whether the asking actually works. Without it, both cognitive strengths bottleneck at the interface: the leader knows what they want, knows whether AI got it right, but cannot consistently produce the output they need.

AI Skills is explicitly distinct from Thinking Framework. Thinking is cognitive; AI Skills is operational. Merging them collapses the APT model and obscures where the actual bottleneck sits in any given leader's profile.

2.2 Four Capability Clusters

Four clusters operate across all three modes of AI Skills. They are not stages — they are skill dimensions every practitioner develops in parallel as they progress through the stack.

ClusterWhat It Means
Prompt, Context & Agentic Engineering DesignMoving beyond simple requests to architecting the entire interaction surface — from prompt to context payload to agentic flow.
AI Capability MappingKnowing what current AI excels at and where it fails. The meta-skill of when to delegate to AI and when to keep human.
Algorithmic Workflow IntegrationEmbedding AI into daily operations — turning disjointed tasks into augmented processes that scale across teams.
Strategic Prompt SequencingChaining AI interactions — decomposition, sequencing, validation, recomposition into a coherent end-state.
2.3 The Three Modes · Concentric, Not Parallel

AI Skills is built as a stacked architecture. Each outer mode contains and depends on the inner ones. A practitioner operating at the ATN Prompt mode is still actively using CIS Prompt techniques, which are still grounded in ACTIONS fundamentals.

MODE 1 · CORE

Mastery moves outward; foundation moves inward. You cannot do ATN Prompt well if your CIS Prompt is shallow. You cannot do CIS Prompt well if your ACTIONS mechanics are weak.

Chapter 03 · The Stack

The Three Modes

ACTIONS · CIS Prompt · ATN Prompt — concentric, not parallel.

01

ACTIONS

Mode 1 · Core · Framework: Prompt Engineering

"How do I get a usable answer in a single interaction?"

3a.1 What ACTIONS Is

ACTIONS covers the mechanics of the prompt itself: how the human formulates a request to an AI model and gets a usable response in a single interaction. Most leaders begin here. The failure mode at this level is treating AI as a search engine — typing fragments instead of structured requests.

ACTIONS is an acronym for the seven elements that structure every prompt — all of them inline within the prompt body itself.

3a.2 The Seven Elements
LetterElementWhat it specifies
AActorWho the AI should act as — the persona, expertise level, voice.
CContextThe inline situational ground — what the AI needs to know for this turn.
TTargetThe task itself — what the AI is being asked to do.
IIntentionThe purpose behind the task — why this matters strategically.
OOutputFormat, structure, length, tone of the deliverable.
NNegativesWhat to avoid — constraints, anti-patterns, boundaries.
SSequencesThe order of multi-part requests, or how outputs chain.
3a.3 Application Example

A leader needs to draft a board update on Q1 performance.

At the ACTIONS layer, they write a structured prompt that specifies Actor (finance analyst voice), Context (Q1 actual vs. budget data), Target (one-page memo), Intention (defend the variance to the board), Output (three sections, no hedging), Negatives (no jargon, no caveats), Sequences (executive summary first, then drill-down). They iterate twice. They get a usable draft in five minutes.

Mastery at ACTIONS means the leader can reliably get a quality single-shot answer when the problem fits within a single interaction. Beyond that, they need CIS Prompt.

02

CIS Prompt

Mode 2 · Envelope · Framework: Context Engineering

"How do I turn prompts from isolated requests into reusable systems of intent?"

3b.1 The Graduation

CIS Prompt wraps the ACTIONS-level prompt inside a deliberately engineered context payload. Three of the seven ACTIONS elements graduate from inline-in-the-prompt to engineered-as-attached-template:

  • C — Context graduates into Context Engineering — versioned reference materials, structured knowledge.
  • I — Intention graduates into Intention Engineering — articulated strategic, transformational, systemic purpose.
  • O — Output graduates into Specification Engineering — precise acceptance bars, format constraints, quality criteria.

The four remaining elements (A, T, N, S) stay inline in the prompt body. They will graduate later — but only in Mode 3.

3b.2 CIS Components
ComponentRoleAnchor Principle
ContextThe factual and situational ground the AI operates onStrip assumptions; provide raw truth
IntentionThe strategic purpose behind the interactionHuman Always human-written — non-negotiable
SpecificationThe precise output requirements: format, constraints, acceptance barSpecificity determines output quality

Intention is structured around three thinking levels: Strategic (the long-horizon goal), Transformational (the possibility that did not exist before AI), and Systemic (the cross-functional second-order effects).

3b.3 Meta Prompting · The Optimization Loop

Inside CIS Prompt sits Meta Prompting: the iterative practice of using AI to critique, refine, and stress-test the prompt itself before and during execution. This is the mechanism by which CIS payloads improve across cycles rather than degrading into stale templates.

3b.4 Tool Selection · A CIS Engineering Decision

Choosing the right AI model for a CIS Prompt is itself an engineering decision driven by the Context Payload, Intention complexity, and Specification requirements. It is not a basic ACTIONS-level concern.

CriterionWhen it matters
Reasoning vs. general-purposeComplex multi-step Specification → reasoning model
Long-context vs. fastHeavy Context Payload → long-context model
Public vs. enterprise-deployedSensitive data in Context → enterprise-deployed instance
Privacy / Cost / Speed / ControlTrade-offs encoded in the Inference Policy (see ATN)
3b.5 Application Example

Same leader. Now the task is strategic: "Help us decide whether to enter the East Java mid-tier segment."

At CIS Prompt level, they construct a Context Payload (market data, competitive intelligence, internal capabilities, regulatory constraints), articulate Intention (this is a 3-year strategic bet, not a tactical pilot), and write a Specification (compare three entry models against five criteria, flag what the analysis cannot resolve). They run a Meta Prompting loop: "What did you miss? What assumptions are weakest?" They select a long-context reasoning model fit for the payload. The output is decision-grade.

CIS layers attach to prompts — they do not embed inside them. The Context Payload is a deliberate, versioned artifact, separate from the live conversation. This separation of concerns is what makes CIS Prompt scalable across teams and use cases.

03

ATN Prompt

Mode 3 · Outer Shell · Framework: Agentic Engineering

"How do I design AI workflows that run with delegated authority — safely?"

3c.1 The Graduation

ATN Prompt wraps CIS Prompt inside a system where AI executes autonomously across multiple steps, calls tools, and operates within a governance envelope defined by humans. Three more ACTIONS elements graduate:

  • A — Actor graduates into Actor Engineering — agent identity, role boundaries, and the Inference Policy (Privacy · Cost · Speed · Control).
  • T — Target graduates into Target Engineering — outcome specifications and Acceptance Criteria for autonomous work.
  • N — Negatives graduates into Negatives Engineering — Approval Gates, hard stops, and the boundaries of autonomous action.

S — Sequences is delegated to the agent: once the agent is well-bounded, it determines its own step order. C, I, O are inherited from CIS layer — they don't graduate again.

3c.2 Four Human-Controlled Elements · Non-Delegable

Regardless of how much execution is delegated, four elements remain permanently under human control. These are not friction points — they are the design requirements that make agentic AI safe and effective.

ElementWhy It Cannot Be Delegated
Human IntentionThe strategic purpose behind any AI workflow must originate from human judgment. AI does not set its own goals.
Human Approval GatesDecision points where human authorization is required before AI proceeds. These define the boundaries of autonomous action.
Human Acceptance CriteriaThe standards by which AI output is judged satisfactory. Only humans with domain expertise can define what "good" looks like.
Human Inference PolicyThe rules governing how AI should reason, what assumptions are permitted, and Privacy/Cost/Speed/Control trade-offs.

Human-in-the-loop governance is not a limitation of agentic AI — it is the design requirement that makes agentic AI safe and effective.

3c.3 The Dual-Mode Slider

ATN Prompt operates on a spectrum of autonomy, from fully manual (every step approved) to fully agentic (the system runs end-to-end within its governance envelope). The slider is not a maturity rating — it is a task-fit decision.

ModeDescriptionWhen to Use
Manual endAI suggests, human executes each stepNovel domains, high-stakes decisions, low trust in model on this task class
HybridAI executes; human approves at gatesMature use cases with defined acceptance criteria
Agentic endAI executes end-to-end; human reviews outcomesHigh-volume, well-bounded tasks with strong Inference Policy and post-hoc auditability
3c.4 Application Example

The same strategic decision is now scaled.

The leader configures an agentic workflow that runs monthly competitive intelligence sweeps across five markets. The workflow uses CIS payloads as its core, executes data collection and synthesis autonomously, but stops at Approval Gates before sending findings to the executive team. Acceptance Criteria — "must flag any regulatory change with potential P&L impact >IDR 5B" — are encoded in the Inference Policy. ATN Prompt has converted what was a quarterly manual effort into a continuous capability.

Chapter 04 · The Other Two Dimensions

Thinking & Domain Expertise

The cognitive layer and the reality check — wrapping around AI Skills.

4.1 Thinking Framework · The Cognitive Layer

The Thinking Framework dimension is the cognitive architecture required to operate AI Skills well. Where AI Skills is operational and technical, Thinking Framework is cognitive: it shapes how the leader thinks when engaging with AI.

Four frameworks form a progression, each grounding a specific mode of AI Skills:

FrameworkGroundsConcept
Algorithmic EmpathyACTIONSTalk to AI as a human (NLP), respect that it is an LLM (probabilistic, no implicit org knowledge, confident-but-not-always-right).
Dialectical SparringCIS · Meta PromptingUse AI for productive friction — not consensus. Challenge, refine, critique, iterate.
Synthetic Sense-MakingCIS · Context + IntentionFuse AI's data-processing with human domain nuance and strategic vision to build cohesive world models.
Systemic Agentic ThinkingATN PromptShift from individual productivity to architecting ecosystems where humans and autonomous agents operate seamlessly.

The progression names where each framework becomes primary, not where it stops mattering. A leader at the ATN layer is still using Algorithmic Empathy, Dialectical Sparring, and Synthetic Sense-Making — they have not graduated past them.

4.3 Domain Expertise · The Reality Check

Domain Expertise is the professional "reality check" you bring to AI. It is the application of your industry knowledge, organizational experience, and professional intuition to guide the AI — ensuring its outputs are not just logically smart, but workable, highly relevant, and up to the highest standards of your field.

Dual Mode

ModeRoleDE LevelAI Skills Range
ProductionEditorHighFull stack — including ATN (bounded by Trust Filter)
ExplorationStudentLowACTIONS + CIS with heavy Meta Prompting; minimal ATN delegation

Caution: Exploration Mode is for learning, not for decision-making in unfamiliar domains. When stakes are real and DE is low, bring in an expert or upgrade to Production Mode via partnership. Do not stretch Exploration Mode to cover decisions it was not designed to support.

4.4 The Four Filters · In Production Mode

When leaders operate in Production Mode, they apply their Domain Expertise through four sequential filters. Each catches a different class of failure that the previous filters miss.

#FilterCatchesAI Skills AnchorCompensable?
1RelevanceGeneric output that ignores org contextCIS Context PayloadPartially
2ExcellenceCompetent but not world-class outputCIS Specification + ATN Acceptance CriteriaPartially
3RealityLogically perfect but operationally impossibleCIS Specification + ATN Inference PolicyPartially
4TrustHuman relational territory misread or skippedATN Approval Gates (where AI stops)No

Relevance → Excellence → Reality → Trust. Each filter narrows the gap between AI output and Business Impact. The first three are partially compensable through CIS engineering and ATN governance. The fourth — Trust — is not. It is the irreducible human contribution that closes the loop from AI output to measurable Business Impact.

Chapter 06 · The Non-Negotiables

The 9 Key Principles of AI-Skills

The rules that hold the architecture together. Non-negotiable.

These nine principles are the bedrock of the AI Skills architecture. Drop any one and the rest start to wobble. They are the standard by which every training module, every advisory engagement, and every internal artifact is checked.

Principle 01
AI Skills is the Execution Layer — distinct from Thinking
AI Skills is operational and technical. Thinking Framework is cognitive. Merging them collapses APT and hides the real bottleneck in any leader's profile.
Principle 02
The three APT dimensions are multiplicative — not additive
APT = f(AI Skills × DE × TF). Weakness in any one dimension suppresses the whole. You cannot compensate with strength in the others.
Principle 03
The Three Modes are concentric, not parallel
ACTIONS ⊂ CIS Prompt ⊂ ATN Prompt. Each outer mode contains and depends on the inner ones. Mastery moves outward; foundation moves inward.
Principle 04
The same seven ACTIONS underlie all three Modes
A · C · T · I · O · N · S. What changes across modes is where each element lives — inline, graduated, inherited, or delegated. The fundamentals don't disappear.
Principle 05
AI is an NLP model — talk to it as a human, respect that it is an LLM
Natural language in, structured discipline beneath. The LLM has world knowledge but no implicit grasp of your organization. It is probabilistic, confident-but-not-always-right.
Principle 06
CIS layers attach to prompts — they do not embed inside them
Context · Intention · Specification are engineered, versioned artifacts separate from the live prompt. Separation of concerns is not stylistic — it is what makes CIS scalable.
Principle 07
Intention is always human-written — across CIS and ATN
AI does not set its own goals. Intention is the strategic anchor that originates from human judgment — both inside CIS Prompt and inside ATN Prompt workflows.
Principle 08
Govern the envelope, not every step
In ATN Prompt, humans control Intention, Approval Gates, Acceptance Criteria, and Inference Policy — the envelope. Step-by-step execution is the agent's job. This is the design that makes agentic AI safe and effective.
Principle 09
The Trust Filter is irreducible
Relational impact, psychological safety, and stakeholder trust cannot be delegated to AI. The Trust Filter defines where AI delegation must end — even in Production Mode, even with strong DE.

Use these as the audit checklist. Before shipping any AI capability — internal or client-facing — run it past all nine. If any principle is being bent, surface it explicitly and decide whether the trade-off is conscious or accidental.
